Ordering a Container of Roofing Sheets? 5 Questions to Ask Your Supplier First

By Henan Wudi New Materials Co., Ltd. · November 2026 · 5 min read

A full container of roofing sheets is real money — and your reputation in front of your own customers rides on the quality inside that box. Before you wire the deposit, run any supplier through these five questions. A serious factory answers all of them without hesitation; a middleman or a cutting-corner factory starts going quiet.

1Are you the factory or a trading company? Can I see the production line live?

A true manufacturer will offer a live video tour of the workshop without being asked twice — co-extrusion lines running, finished tiles stacked, forklifts loading containers. Ask them to show your exact model being produced. Trading companies cannot do this, and when a quality problem appears you want to be talking to the people who operate the machines, not to another agent.

2What is the layer structure, and how much real ASA is in the surface?

This is where cheap tiles cheat. Quality synthetic resin tiles are co-extruded multi-layer sheets — an ASA surface layer for UV and color stability, plus a PVC/ASA composite core. Some suppliers cut the ASA layer down or substitute plain PVC, and the tile fades to chalk within 2–3 years — which lands on you, the importer, when your customers complain. Ask for the layer structure (e.g. AB or ABCD) in writing, plus UV accelerated aging test data.

3What warranty do you offer, and is it in writing on the documents?

A vague promise of "very good quality" is worth nothing. A factory confident in its product states a specific warranty — for example 30 years service life — and backs it with fire rating certificates (B1) and load test results (e.g. 150 kg without crack at 660 mm support distance). Get these documents with your Proforma Invoice; your local buyers will ask you for them too.

4Can I mix models and colors in one container, and what exactly fits?

For a first order, most distributors mix two or three colors of one model plus ridge tiles and accessories. A good supplier sends back an exact loading plan — pieces, bundles, weight and a packing sketch — and after loading, photos and video of the packed container before the ship sails. If a supplier cannot tell you how many m² fit in your container, they have not loaded containers before.

5What are the payment and shipping terms?

Standard practice in this industry: T/T, typically a deposit with the balance before shipment, and pricing quoted as FOB (you handle freight) or CIF (they arrange shipping to your port). Ask who handles export customs clearance — the factory should. And confirm the lead time in weeks, not "soon". Everything should appear on the PI before you pay anything.

Bonus question: "Can you send free samples?" A real factory sends samples for free — you pay only the courier fee, usually deductible from the first order. Holding the real tile in your hand before committing to a container is the cheapest insurance in this business.
