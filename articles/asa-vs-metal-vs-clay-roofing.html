ASA Resin Tile vs. Metal Roofing vs. Clay Tile — Which Roofing Is Best for Hot Climates?

By Henan Wudi New Materials Co., Ltd. · November 2026 · 6 min read

If you import or distribute roofing sheets in Southeast Asia, Africa, the Middle East or Latin America, you have probably been asked the same question by contractors again and again: resin tile, metal sheet, or clay — which one should I put on the roof?

The honest answer depends on the climate, the building type and the budget. Here is a practical comparison from a factory floor perspective — not a sales pitch.

Why ASA resin tiles win in hot and coastal markets

1. The roof stays cooler

ASA resin conducts heat at only 0.325 W/(m·K) — a fraction of steel. Under the same midday sun, rooms under resin tile roofs stay noticeably cooler than rooms under metal sheets, cutting the need for an extra insulation layer or heavier air-conditioning use.

2. No rust, ever

In coastal cities and industrial zones, the No.1 complaint about metal roofing is corrosion — screw holes rust first, then leak. ASA resin is a polymer: acid rain, salt spray and alkalis simply do not react with it. This is why resin tiles are popular for farms, chemical workshops and seaside buildings.

3. Color that survives the sun

The ASA surface layer (acrylonitrile-styrene-acrylate) is the same engineering polymer used in automotive exterior parts precisely because it resists UV degradation. Cheap PVC sheets fade to chalk within a few years; a proper ASA tile keeps its color for decades — a strong selling point for your retail customers.

Where metal and clay still make sense

  • Metal sheets: large-span industrial warehouses where structure cost dominates and insulation is handled separately, and where lowest upfront price wins the bid.
  • Clay tiles: heritage and traditional-style architecture where the look is required by regulation or customer taste — but expect high transport cost and breakage rates.
Wholesale tip: many distributors stock ASA resin tiles alongside metal sheets. Resin tile wins the residential, villa, resort and flat-to-slope renovation jobs — the segments where end customers pay more for comfort, quiet and appearance.

What to check before you order

Not all "resin tiles" are equal. The ASA surface layer is the expensive part — some factories cut it down or replace it with plain PVC, and the tile fades in 2–3 years. Before ordering a container, ask the supplier for the layer structure (AB or ABCD co-extrusion), UV aging test data and the warranty terms in writing.
