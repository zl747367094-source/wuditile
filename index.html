Core Advantages

ASA Engineering Resin — Built to Outlast Ordinary Roofing

ASA ternary polymer (acrylonitrile + styrene + acrylate) surface layer resists UV degradation, aging and fading — service life over 30 years, proven by laboratory UV accelerated aging tests.

☀️

Durable & Natural Colors

Weather-resistant modified ASA resin keeps color stable under UV, moisture, heat and cold — suitable for permanent building roofs.

🛡️

Excellent Corrosion Resistance

No chemical reaction with acids, alkalis or salts — ideal for acid-rain areas, coastal regions and corrosive factories.

🌡️

Thermal Insulation

Thermal conductivity 0.325 W/(m·K), far lower than clay, cement and color steel tiles — good insulation even without extra insulation layer.

🔊

Sound Insulation

Three-layer composite core structure blocks external noise — quiet and comfortable during heavy rain.

🔥

Flame Retardant B1

Fire resistance meets B1 level of GB/T 8624-2012 classification for burning behavior of building materials.

💪

Load & Impact Resistant

No crack or damage under 150 kg load at 660 mm support distance — withstands year-round snow cover without breaking.

⚡

Insulating & Lightning Safe

The material is an insulator that does not conduct electricity, even in thunderstorm weather — high safety factor.

💧

Self-Cleaning Surface

Smooth and dense surface, absorbs no water or dust — rain washes it clean, snow slides off easily.

🌱

Green & Eco-Friendly

Non-toxic, recyclable, meets green building standards — lightweight, waterproof, flame retardant and insulating all in one.
